The Dynamic Duo

Nine trips to Reggie Mush's channel, about 590,000 views, and one friend teaching another how to bet.

October 2026 · By CZVegas · 9 min read

Reggie Mush and Mike have been friends since long before either of them made casino videos. So when Reggie launched his YouTube channel in January 2026, the first person he brought on was Mike: a 10-year Strip dealer he introduced as Chicago Mike. Viewers started calling him Dealer Mike, and the two of them got a nickname in the comments too: "the dynamic duo craps guys."

Here's every guest appearance so far, what Mike taught in each one, and the moments people still bring up.

#1Reggie's first guest ever

1. The first one

January 2026 · "I Paid an Ex-Dealer to Help Me Win at the Casino" · 101K views

Reggie's very first video. Mike walked through what to do with $100 (pass line, inside numbers, collect before you press) and then the $122 inside: $25 on the 5 and 9, $36 on the 6 and 8, go to what it pays on the first hit. When Mike picked up the dice, the 9 went from $25 to $750, one step from table max. The full story is in Where It Started.

2. Mondays with Mike: the come bet

February 2026 · "Trying the BEST Craps Strategy of All Time" · 41K views

Reggie opened by saying the comments were full of people who'd tried "Mike's dealer strategy" and gone on runs, so he brought him back. This time Mike taught a come bet strategy: $15 on the pass line with $30 odds, then $15 come bets with odds until you have three numbers working. When a come number hits again, you raise the odds instead of adding another bet. It builds slowly, so a quick seven-out doesn't hurt much. The table was ice cold, but the closing advice stuck: "When you're getting in a car, you don't just start driving. Figure out your plan and then get to your plan."

3. Crapless, and the button

March 2026 · "A Ex-Dealer Showed Me How To WIN Big On Craps!!!" · 203K views

The big one. Reggie opened with "Chicago Mike's back," and Mike brought a crapless version of the pattern: $262 across, with the 2 and 12 pressed together and the 3 and 11 pressed together ("These are brothers," Reggie decided). Mike also accidentally tapped the bubble craps button mid-roll and ended a good shooter's run. On the next machine, someone else's finger turned $1,000 into roughly $5,300.

4. Scaling the pattern up and down

March 2026 · "I Tried the Best Craps Strategy in the Casino" · 49K views

The comments kept asking: what if I don't have $122? What if I want to bet $220 or $320 inside? So at El Cortez they showed both. On bubble craps they started with about $47 in action and climbed into the pattern one unit at a time. On the live table they started at $220 inside plus the 4 and 10. Mike's explanation: "It's like you're an airplane. You're waiting to take off on the runway." You can start anywhere. The numbers in the pattern just tell you what to do once you get there. As they put it: "These aren't rigid rules. They're more like guidelines."

5. ATS Sunday

April 2026 · "I Brought a Craps PRO to Help Me WIN!" · 35K views

Reggie's weekly All Tall Small chase, with reinforcements. On Mike's roll they went "from Iron Cross to all the way across." On a stranger's hot roll they ran the $122 inside alongside Reggie's own "hit then split it" regression, which Reggie named the Mike and Reggie strategy. (Bert and Ernie was proposed and rejected.) After all of that, they cashed out almost exactly even. Up $9, plus Mike got a fancy bottled water.

6. The blackjack episode

April 2026 · "I Brought a Blackjack PRO to Help Me Win!" · 28K views

Mike dealt blackjack too, so Reggie took him to the tables. His betting system is simple: after a win, let the red chips ride and pull back the green, so the bet grows on a run while the bankroll grows too. After any loss, go straight back to the base bet. On close calls against a dealer's 2, his advice was to decide whether you think a 2 plays more like an ace or more like a 3, then always play it that way. Mike turned about $300 into roughly $900. Reggie split tens. It did not go well.

7. A regression into the pattern

April 2026 · "I Tried The BEST Craps Strategy to Make You Money" · 33K views

ATS Sunday, on a Monday, on a crapless table. Mike's idea: start at a quarter on every number, cut each one down after its first hit to lock up profit, then step into the Fibonacci pattern once everything is paid for. Reggie called it "the classic Dealer Mike, Chicago Mike pattern, also known as the Fibonacci sequence if you're fancy." They doubled $1,000 into $2,000, and someone bought in mid-roll, which led to Mike quoting his own song: "Don't you buy in the middle of my roll."

8. The biggest hand pay

May 2026 · "I Just Won My BIGGEST Hand Pay EVER!" · 68K views

They opened with the "classic Dealer Mike strat" on bubble craps and turned it into a profit, then wandered over to Destroyer Naval Strike, the Battleship dice game. One missile run later, Reggie had the biggest hand pay of his life, and they walked out up about $3,600.

9. Two hits, then split

August 2026 · "I Had an Ex-Dealer Help Me Make Money at Craps!" · 30K views

By now the two of them were building strategies together. Mike had been watching Reggie's hit-and-split and brought a twist: go across big, collect two hits, then cut everything in half and start pressing into the Fibonacci numbers. It's the same idea that became the Two Hit Split on the CZVegas channel. Mike also had a rough day with the dice and announced he was done shooting on Reggie's channel. His summary: "When I came on Reggie's channel and showed him Fibonacci, he took that and tried a bunch of other stuff... two brains are better than one."
