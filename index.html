About

Mike Czmil, a.k.a. Chicago Mike, grew up in La Grange Park, Illinois, spent some quality years at LTHS, and refuses to say what he did for work during that time. After school he went legit in the most literal way possible: a career as a certified pharmacy technician at Walgreens. Then he traded the pharmacy counter for the craps table and landed at Paris Las Vegas, where he was considered one of the best dealers on the Strip. These days he sells houses with the Crighton Rinaldi Team and has called Vegas home for about 15 years. The desert hasn't managed to get rid of him yet.

Today he is a content creator going by the moniker CZVegas on YouTube and Instagram, teaching craps strategy and covering everything else Vegas: gambling, food, travel and the occasional questionable decision. URComped even put CZVegas on its Top 100 Gaming Influencers list, which he would like you to know about.

The Road to Vegas

Pills, chips, houses, content. In that order.

  1. La Grange Park, Illinois

    Certified Pharmacy Technician

    Started at Walgreens and became a certified pharmacy technician. Learned to count fast and accurately. This becomes important later.

  2. North Carolina

    Senior Technician, Statewide

    Climbed to senior pharmacy technician, then traveling senior tech running flu vaccine distribution across the entire state. In his spare time he stood in the background of One Tree Hill and other movies and shows, acting his heart out where nobody could see it.

  3. Naperville, Illinois

    Trainer, Scheduler, Hospital Pharmacy

    Moved back to Illinois with Walgreens, training technicians across the district and filling in as district scheduler, the job everyone complains about and nobody wants. Then on to Edwards Hospital in Naperville, preparing intrathecal chemotherapy for pediatric patients.

  4. Joliet, Illinois

    Craps Dealer

    Traded the pill counter for a craps table at Harrah's Joliet, where he was considered one of the best dealers in the building. Turns out counting pills and paying bets aren't that different.

  5. Las Vegas, Nevada

    The Strip

    Transferred to Paris and Bally's Las Vegas, where again he was considered one of the best dealers to ever grace the felt. Ask him. He'll tell you.

  6. Las Vegas, Nevada

    Realtor and Creator

    Got his real estate license and joined the Crighton Rinaldi Team. Produced SuperVegasLife, launched CZVegas, and now juggles houses and dice at the same time.

Community

In 2014 Mike organized Nerds Against Cancer, a Magic: The Gathering charity tournament in Henderson, Nevada, that raised thousands of dollars toward a local veteran's cancer treatment. Never underestimate nerds with a cause.

He also serves as a lay member of the State Bar of Nevada's Southern Nevada Disciplinary Board, which hears misconduct complaints against Nevada attorneys. Each panel has two attorneys and one member of the public, sitting much like jurors to decide whether discipline is warranted. Mike is the one who isn't a lawyer, which he considers his strongest qualification.
